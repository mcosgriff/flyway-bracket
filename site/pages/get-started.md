There are three things to do before you play.

## 1. Your name and home county

Choose a display name. It is the name the other players in your league will see. It does not have to be
your real name.

Then choose your home county. You pick your state from a list, then your county.

Your home county matters. It decides which birds you can be expected to find. [Fair play across the
country](fair-play.html) explains why.

## 2. Download your state's birds

The app needs to know which birds are around you, week by week. That information comes in a "pack".

A pack covers one state. It holds every county in that state, and the birds seen in each county at each
time of year.

Open the Packs tab and download the pack for your state. After that, the pack is saved on your phone. It
works with no signal.

Going on a trip? Download the pack for that state too.

## 3. Log your first outing

An outing is one trip outside to look for birds. A walk in the park counts. So does an hour at a lake.

1. On the Home screen, tap "Start outing".
2. The app will ask to use your location. It uses it to tell which county you are in. Your exact position
   stays on your phone.
3. When you see a bird, add it to the outing.
4. When you are done, end the outing.

You do not need an account to log outings. You do not need a league either.
