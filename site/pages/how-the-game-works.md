## What a bracket is

A bracket is a knockout contest. Two sides meet, one wins, and the winner moves on to meet another
winner. This goes on until one side is left.

In Flyway Bracket the sides are birds. A meeting of two birds is called a matchup.

## The 64 birds

Each season, your league gets 64 migrating birds. This is called the field.

The field is split into four quarters. Each quarter has 16 birds.

## Seeds

Each bird in a quarter gets a number from 1 to 16. This number is its seed.

Seeds come from how often each bird is reported in the home counties of your league's players during the
season. Seed 1 is the bird most players are likely to see. Seed 16 is the long shot.

In the first week, the likely birds meet the long shots. Seed 1 meets seed 16, seed 2 meets seed 15, and
so on down to seed 8 against seed 9.

Seeds 1 and 2 of a quarter are kept apart. They can only meet in the last matchup of their quarter.

## Your picks

Before the season starts, you fill in the whole bracket. For every matchup, you pick the bird you think
will win.

There are 63 matchups in all: 32 in the first week, then 16, 8, 4, 2 and 1.

Your picks lock at midnight at the start of the first Monday, in the league's time zone. After that you
cannot change them.

Other players cannot see your picks until they lock.

## The six weekly rounds

A season has six rounds. Each round lasts one week, from Monday through Sunday.

| Week | Round | Birds still in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Round of 64 | 64 |
| 2 | Round of 32 | 32 |
| 3 | Round of 16 | 16 |
| 4 | Quarterfinals | 8 |
| 5 | Semifinals | 4 |
| 6 | Final | 2 |

During the week, you go outside and log the birds you see. A round is decided after its Sunday ends.

## How a matchup is decided

A matchup goes to the bird that more of the league found, counting only players who could have seen it.

Here is the same rule in steps.

1. For each bird, count the players who could have seen it that week. These are its *eligible* players.
2. Count how many of those players logged the bird.
3. Work out the share: players who logged it, out of players who could have.
4. The bird with the higher share wins.

You count once for a bird, however many of it you saw.

If you log a bird that is not expected that week where you live or where you went, it does not count.

[Fair play across the country](fair-play.html) says who is eligible for a bird.

### If the shares are equal

The bird with the better seed wins. The better seed is the lower number.

### If very few players could see a bird

A bird needs at least 3 eligible players for its share to count. With fewer than 3, its share counts as
zero.

This stops a bird from winning because one player could see it and did.

If neither bird has 3 eligible players, the bird with the better seed wins.

## An example

<div class="example">

These numbers are made up. A league has 20 players. In week 1, a seed 5 bird meets a seed 12 bird.

| | Seed 5 bird | Seed 12 bird |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Players who could have seen it | 16 | 5 |
| Players who logged it | 8 | 4 |
| Share | 8 of 16, or 50% | 4 of 5, or 80% |

The seed 12 bird wins. Its share is 80%, and 80% is higher than 50%.

More players logged the seed 5 bird: 8 against 4. But only 5 players could have seen the seed 12 bird, and
4 of them found it.

</div>

## Points

You score points for each pick you got right. A wrong pick scores nothing.

Later rounds are worth more. The points double each round.

| Round | Points for a right pick |
| --- | --- |
| Round of 64 | 1 |
| Round of 32 | 2 |
| Round of 16 | 4 |
| Quarterfinals | 8 |
| Semifinals | 16 |
| Final | 32 |

## The upset bonus

An upset is when the bird with the worse seed wins.

If you picked an upset and it happened, you get extra points. The extra is the gap between the two seeds.

In the example above, seed 12 beat seed 5. The gap is 12 minus 5, which is 7. A player who picked the
seed 12 bird scores 1 point for the round, plus 7 for the upset. That is 8 points.
