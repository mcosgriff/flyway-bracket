Flyway Bracket is a bird-watching game for Android phones. Each migration season, 64 migrating birds are
set against each other in a bracket, and you pick the winners. Then you and your league go outside, and
each matchup goes to the bird that more of you found.

## Where things stand

The app is in early development. Nothing is released yet.

Today the app has a Home screen and little else. You cannot yet log an outing, join a league or fill in a
bracket.

Pages about things the app cannot do yet start with a "Coming in the beta" note.
