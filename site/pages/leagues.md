A league is the group of people you play with. Everyone in a league shares one bracket of 64 birds.

The players in a league can live anywhere. They do not have to live near each other.

## Before you join

Leagues need the internet. Players' picks and sightings have to reach a place that every phone in the
league can read.

So the first time you open the League tab, the app shows a page. The page says what will be sent, and
asks if you agree. Nothing is sent until you say yes.

[Your privacy](privacy.html) has the full list.

## Three ways into a league

### Join with a code

A friend who is in a league can send you its invite code. Type the code into the app to join.

### Be placed in an open league

No code? Ask the app to place you. It adds you to an open league that has room.

### Create your own

Anyone can create a league. You give it a name, and you choose its time zone. You then get an invite code
to share with your friends.

You can also open your league to players who have no league.

## How many players

A league needs at least 8 players before its bracket can start.

A league can hold up to 64 players.

## When a season starts

There are two seasons a year, one for each bird migration. A season that starts in January through June
is the spring season. A season that starts later in the year is the fall season.

A season always starts on a Monday.

It then runs for six weeks. [How the game works](how-the-game-works.html) says what happens in each week.

All of a league's dates use the league's time zone. That is the one chosen when the league was created.
