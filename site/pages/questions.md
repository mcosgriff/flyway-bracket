## Common questions

### Can I play now?

Not yet. The app is in early development, and nothing is released. Today it has a Home screen and little
else.

### Will there be an iPhone version?

Flyway Bracket is an Android app. No iPhone version is planned.

### Does it cost anything?

No. There are no ads either.

### Do I need to know birds well?

No. The game only asks you to go outside and note the birds you can name.

### Do I need an account?

Not to log outings. To play in a league you will need an account, but it is anonymous. It has no email
and no password. [Your privacy](privacy.html) says more.

### Where does the bird information come from?

The plan is to build it from [eBird](https://ebird.org). eBird is a website where birders around the
world report what they see. It is run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Using eBird's information in a released app needs the Cornell Lab's permission. The project does not have
that permission yet.

Flyway Bracket is not affiliated with eBird or the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

### Why did Android warn me about installing the app?

Android warns about every app that does not come from the Play Store. [Install](install.html#the-warning-from-android)
explains what the warning means.

### A bird won its matchup, but hardly anyone saw it. Why?

A matchup counts only the players who could have seen each bird. A bird that few players could see can
win when most of those few found it.

[How the game works](how-the-game-works.html#an-example) has an example.
[Fair play across the country](fair-play.html) says why the game works this way.

### I do not live where that bird lives. Does that hurt me?

No. You are not counted for a bird you could not have seen.

### What happens if I lose my phone?

In the beta, you will lose your account, your place in your league and your points.
[Your privacy](privacy.html#if-you-lose-your-phone) says why.

## Report a problem

Problems are reported on GitHub, the website where the app's code is kept. You need a free GitHub account.

[Open the report page](https://github.com/mcosgriff/flyway-bracket/issues/new/choose) and choose the form
that fits:

- **Something is broken.** The app crashed, froze, or did something it should not do.
- **A result looks wrong.** The wrong bird won a matchup, or your points look wrong.
- **I have an idea.** Something is hard to do, or something is missing.

Reports are public. Anyone can read them. So leave out your location, your invite code and your account
details.

### A security or privacy problem

Do not report these in public. Use the
[private form](https://github.com/mcosgriff/flyway-bracket/security/advisories/new). Only the maintainer
can read it.

One person looks after Flyway Bracket in their own time. A reply may take a while.
